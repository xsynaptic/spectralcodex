## Heading level two

Headings size in `em` against the body, so they scale with the prose step rather than the type
scale. Vietnamese diacritics stack tall enough to test the leading: *Nhà thờ Đức Bà Sài Gòn, xây
dựng từ năm 1863 đến 1880.* Chinese runs wrap by phrase rather than by character:
